**Summary:** The GALE gene encodes UDP-galactose-4-epimerase, an enzyme that catalyzes two similar reactions: converting UDP-glucose to UDP-galactose, and converting UDP-N-acetylglucosamine to UDP-N-acetylgalactosamine. 

* catalyzes UDP-glucose <-> UDP-galactose

* UDP-N-acetylglucosamine <-> UDP-N-acetylgalactosamine

Because the enzyme does both, cells or individuals with mutations depend on external sources of both galactose and N-acetylgalactosamine, which are needed to build glycoproteins and glycolipids. Mutations in GALE cause **epimerase-deficiency galactosemia (galactosemia type 3)**, with symptoms such as liver damage, early-onset cataracts, deafness and cognitive disability, ranging from mild (“peripheral”) to severe (“generalized”). Several alternatively spliced transcripts encode the same protein.
